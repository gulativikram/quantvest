About Us

Quant-vest Investment & Portfolio Management

Quant-vest is a Portfolio Management Firm that focuses on generating returns by investing in a diversified portfolio of high quality publicly listed Indian companies using a combination of Value Investing Approach along with Advanced Mathematical Models. Company's primary goal is to generate returns that superceed Index Returns while ensuring a robust Risk Management framework to protect Capital.

Value Investing Approach

Identifying robust fundamentals and intrinsic valuation discounts across high-quality publicly listed Indian equities.

Advanced Mathematical Models

Deploying quantitative algorithms to identify alpha signals and systematically rebalance risk exposure in real time.

Capital Protection

Embedding strict risk metrics and downside controls within every portfolio strategy to safeguard capital against drawdowns.
